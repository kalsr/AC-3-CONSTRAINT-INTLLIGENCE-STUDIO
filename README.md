The AC-3 Constraint Intelligence Studio, built by Randy Singh of Kalsnet (KNet) Consulting, takes on complex puzzles and planning challenges and delivers answers that obey every rule, every time. Its secret is simple but powerful: it eliminates impossible options before it ever guesses, cutting problems down to size with a proven method called AC-3. 
One app tackles six real-world domains: 
- Sudoku puzzles, 
- exam and meeting schedules, 
- map colouring and radio channels, 
- PC and car configuration, 
- factory and construction planning, and the 
- interpretation of drawings and sentences.

clear charts and tables show exactly which options were eliminated, which rule removed each one, and proof that the final answer is valid. When the work is done, walk away with a polished, professional report in PDF, 
Word, 
spreadsheet,
